Privacy Policy: Submission Exporter for LeetCode

This extension collects no data.

  • It runs only on https://leetcode.com/*, inside your own logged-in session.
  • It reads your submission history from LeetCode (the same data you see on the website) and builds a ZIP file

    • on your device. The ZIP is saved by your browser. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

  • It has no servers, no analytics, no tracking, no ads and no remote code.
  • It does not read, store or transmit your password or session cookie. Requests use your browser's normal session.
  • It requests no browser permissions beyond access to leetcode.com.

    • Questions or concerns: open an issue on the project's GitHub page.

    Not affiliated with or endorsed by LeetCode.
